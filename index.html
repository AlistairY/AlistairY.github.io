About Me

I’m Leqian Yang, an undergraduate at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), studying artificial intelligence and physics. I am advised by Ziming Liu at Tsinghua University and Liu Ziyin at MIT.

I’m interested in making sense of how AI learns. My research centers on learning dynamics and interpretability: how representations and behaviors emerge during training, and what mechanisms can explain them. I’m especially drawn to complex learning phenomena that might admit a simple, testable explanation, and I enjoy exploring them through theoretical analysis and controlled experiments. My current work approaches these questions through diffusion models and physical world models, representation geometry, and the relationship between data structure and generalization.

I’m always happy to exchange ideas with people who share these interests, whether about ongoing research, a puzzling observation, or a possible collaboration. Feel free to get in touch.

Outside of research, I enjoy cooking, puzzle hunts, running, and badminton.
